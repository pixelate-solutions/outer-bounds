# G — Lab, bring-up and validation equipment (one-time purchases)

Prepared 7 Oct 2026 for Outer Bounds. This list covers the lab and one-time equipment only; per-unit product parts are in the other BOM files. Prices are in USD, before tax and shipping, as captured on the cited page today unless the note says otherwise. calib.io prices are in EUR and were converted at an **assumed 1.17 USD/EUR**. One line is an uncited estimate: the launcher frame allowance.

## Recommendations in brief

- **Ground truth: buy one Certified Pre-Owned Foresight GCQuad with club data ($11,999 from PlayBetter).** It is a camera-based (quadrascopic) unit, the same measurement principle as ours, and it is the industry reference for launch conditions and spin indoors. It is also cheaper than a used TrackMan, which needs a transfer fee and a subscription on top. **Don't buy a TrackMan 4 now.** For outdoor full-flight and carry validation of the radar, rent bay time at a TrackMan range or fitting studio. Buy a TrackMan later only if radar-vs-radar carry comparison becomes a gating requirement (see the "Not in totals" table).
- **Hitting bay:** Carl's Place DIY enclosure and impact screen, pipe frame kit, and a Fiberbuilt Studio mat. **No projector is needed.** We are validating measurements, not running a simulator.
- **Repeatable shots:** no off-the-shelf golf launcher with independent backspin and sidespin control was found. Spinball and JUGS two-wheel machines are sized for baseballs and top out around 104-105 mph. **Recommended: an in-house two-wheel launcher** (2 ODrive S1 + 2 M8325s motors, with the wheel speed difference setting backspin and head tilt setting sidespin), checked against ProChrono light gates and the GCQuad. **Golf Laboratories robot time is quote-only** (golflabs.com publishes no rate), so budget it separately for a final validation campaign.
- **Measuring 50 A strobe current:** use a **coaxial shunt (T&M SDN-414-05, 2 GHz)** plus a **Pearson 2877** (100 A peak, 2 ns rise). A Siglent clamp probe such as the CP5030A ($3,205) costs more and is slower (50 MHz) for this job.
- **Oscilloscope:** Siglent SDS2354X HD (350 MHz, 4 ch, 12-bit, $3,449). The lean option is the Rigol MHO934 (350 MHz, 4 ch, 12-bit) at **$999**, which is very good value. Its main compromise is the 7" screen.
- **Laser/IEC 62471:** we can pre-check 940 nm irradiance with the PM100D2 + S121C through a known aperture. Proper photobiological-safety classification (radiance and thermal-retinal limits) needs a calibrated spectroradiometer and imaging setup, so **a test lab should do it**. That is not in this list.
- **Compute:** RTX 5090 street prices are about $4,400-4,800 for the card alone in Oct 2026. A complete workstation is about $7,765. Cloud RTX 5090 runs $0.69-0.99/hr, so **start in the cloud** unless training runs every day.

## Key caveats and verification flags

1. **GCQuad CPO stock is limited:** PlayBetter says it won't restock once sold out. PlayBetter's page conflicts on the warranty (1-year in the description, 2-year in the box contents), so confirm before buying. Used units bought from a private party need Foresight's $199 transfer fee.
2. **calib.io targets at 940 nm:** the printed black areas may lose contrast in NIR. Test a sample under our 940 nm strobe, or ask calib.io about NIR-optimised printing, before buying all three boards.
3. **e-con IMX900:** we need the **mono** variant (e-CAM37M_CUONX) for 940 nm sensitivity, and it is priced on request. The $249 used here is the color sample price, used as a placeholder.
4. **TI EVMs:** all three show $156.45 on ti.com today (from the page's data-price field). Confirm in the cart.
5. **Radxa Dragon Q6A:** the 16 GB variant ($119.50) is listed cheaper than the 12 GB ($169). That may be a sale or a listing error.
6. **ProChrono with golf balls:** it is designed for projectiles, and its use with a 42.7 mm ball is unverified. Treat it as a launcher-repeatability check only. The GCQuad remains the ground truth.
7. **Launcher speed range:** M8325s motors (100 KV) with 6-8" wheels reach about 38-50 m/s surface speed, which covers wedge to mid-iron ball speeds. Driver-speed launches (70+ m/s) need higher-KV motors or larger wheels, and need guarding.
8. **Memory-driven price inflation (2026):** the Jetson Orin Nano Super is $399 (was $249), the RUBIK Pi 3 is $259, the RTX 5090 is ~2.3x MSRP, and the Synology DS925+ is above its MSRP. Re-check these prices at order time.
9. **Discontinued or last-time-buy:** EPC9126/HC (replaced by EPC9180), Hakko FX-951 (successor FX-971), Thorlabs PM100D (replaced by PM100D2), Total Phase PD analyzer (use CY4500), Infineon Position2Go, Bambu X1C (replaced by X2D / H2D), and Siglent DPB4080 (DPB1300 used instead).
